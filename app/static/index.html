Your CSV needs three columns: date (YYYY-MM-DD), merchant, amount. Expenses are negative; income is positive. Single currency: INR.

How to get started

  1. Import a CSV with date, merchant, and amount.
  2. Use negative amounts for expenses and positive amounts for income.
  3. Save your actual current bank balance separately.

Importing history does not change your balance. Duplicate rows are skipped.
